Common questions

  • Why no tides for my spot? Tides come from NOAA stations on U.S. coasts and territories. Lakes, rivers and spots outside the U.S. have no tide screen; the bite outlook works everywhere Apple Weather does.
  • How is the bite outlook made? From the pressure trend, solunar windows (moon overhead, underfoot, rising and setting), wind, light around sunrise and sunset, and moving tide where there is one.
  • Restore Pro on a new iPhone: Settings → Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Plunko.
