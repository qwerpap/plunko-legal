Privacy Policy

Plunko: Fishing Forecast · Last updated September 27, 2026

Plunko estimates when fish are likely to bite at your spot and keeps a log of your catches. There are no accounts, no server that stores your catches, and we don't sell or share your data.

Location

If you allow location access, Plunko uses your approximate location while the app is in use to request the local weather forecast from Apple Weather and to find the nearest U.S. NOAA tide station. The location is sent to Apple's weather service for that request only; the tide request sends NOAA only a station number. Plunko does not keep a location history and does not send it to us. You can instead search a place by name; saved spots stay on your iPhone.

Weather and tide data

Forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit); Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. Tide predictions come from the public NOAA CO-OPS service for U.S. stations. Moon and sun times are calculated on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Catches you log: species, time, weight, length, bait, note, photo, the spot name and the conditions at that hour.
  • Your saved spots and your answers from setup (species, type of water, how often you fish).

This information is stored only in Plunko's own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Photos you add are picked with the system photo picker; Plunko does not read the rest of your library.

Notifications

The best-window heads-up and the trial reminder are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Plunko receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, purchase events and your setup answers (species, type of water) to validate subscriptions and measure which offers work; it does not receive your location, your catches or your photos.

Analytics and tracking

Plunko does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

Delete a catch with the × next to it in the log, remove a spot with the × in Spots, or delete Plunko from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

A guide, not a guarantee

The bite outlook is an estimate from weather, moon, light and tide. Tide predictions are not for navigation.

Children

Plunko is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
